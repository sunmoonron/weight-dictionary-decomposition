# After the ICLR 2027 reviews

The paper was submitted to ICLR 2027 and withdrawn after three reviews. These runs answer the reviewers' questions with the paper's own scripts and protocol: GPT-2 small, WikiText-2, 32,768 states, FVU on typical states (norm at most ten times the median). Predictions were written to `prereg.json` and `prereg2.json` before any run, smoke tests included; `analyze.py` scores the first round (10 of 15 confirmed). Every rerun of a recorded dictionary reproduces the paper's numbers to three decimals.

- `pr1_provenance_random.py` (Reviewer 7MWE): the Section 4.1 provenance controls next to size-matched random directions (three seeds), each control's own Gram-preserving rotation, and the embedding rows alone. At k = 32, with 19,210 atoms and no embeddings: writers 0.375, non-writers 0.490, random 0.570, rotated non-writers 0.578. On the sink states writers and non-writers both reach 0.0002 at k = 64, random 0.33.
- `pr2_z2_variant.py` (Reviewers NMa5 and MgDZ): `exp_z2_ci.py` with two switches, `WDD_LAYER` and `WDD_RANDOM_INIT`. Layer 2: FVU 0.230 against 0.547 rotated, top-1 recall 0.739. Layer 6: the paper's 0.338 and 0.647. Layer 10: 0.345 against 0.517, recall 0.235. Random weights at layer 6: 0.495 against 0.500, recall 0.008.
- `pr3_oracles_solvers.py` (Reviewers NMa5 and MgDZ, second round, `prereg2.json`): ground truth for the non-MLP atoms of the 64-atom OMP support, and four solvers on the same states. Support on typical states: MLP rows 62%, attention atoms 21%, token rows 17%, position rows 0.1%. Head oracle (each head's true write projected on its own basis atoms): the head with the largest write has an atom in the support for 20% of tokens (28% with centred writes) against 14% for a random head, its selected atoms capture a median of 1% of its write (5% centred), the atom with the largest true attention coefficient is in the support for 3% of tokens; among selected attention atoms the sign matches the true coefficient for 70%, 58% pass the paper's 5% rule against a base rate of 35% over all attention atoms, the correlation with the true coefficient is 0.52 and the recovered coefficient is a median of 4.7 times the true one. Embeddings: a selected token row is the current token in 0.3% of cases and an earlier context token in 1.7% (33% for unigram-matched random tokens); the token's own row is in the support for 3% of tokens and its position row for 0.02%. Solvers, top-1 identification of the largest MLP write and FVU of a refit on 64 atoms: OMP 0.647 and 0.180, one-shot ranking 0.766 and 0.533, one-shot ranking through the dual frame 0.801 and 0.531, LASSO 0.849 and 0.433 (penalty 9.87, tuned; 0.848 at 9.9105).
- `pr4_sae_features.py` (Reviewers MgDZ and 7MWE): all 24,576 decoder directions of the public residual SAE at `blocks.7.hook_resid_pre` decomposed over the layer-6 dictionary, atoms and targets centred over the model dimension to match the SAE's coordinates. The first atom is an MLP row for 93% of features (random directions 54%, covariance-matched directions 62%, typical states 81%); sixteen atoms leave a median of 0.48 of a feature unexplained (rotated dictionary 0.72, random directions 0.72, covariance-matched directions 0.63, typical states 0.47).
- `analyze2.py` scores the second round (19 of 19 confirmed; output in `results/analysis2.txt`). Each of these quantities had been measured before on a different pipeline in follow-up experiments, and the reruns reproduce those numbers to two decimals. `make_note_blocks.py` builds the note's paragraphs from the result files.
- `run_pr.sh`, `chain_gpu0.sh`, `chain_gpu1.sh`, `chain2_gpu0.sh`, `chain2_gpu1.sh`: how they were run on two 8 GB cards; `results/` holds every output, the per-token records and the logs.

To rescore, run `python post_review/analyze.py`. It computes the typical-state numbers from the per-token records itself, so `scripts/exp_z2_recompute.py` is not needed.
